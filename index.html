Who we are

We make coffee for the people who love to drink it.

Cafe Mayfair sits in the Woolworths Centre, behind the timber signage everyone in Durban North knows. Inside it's warm oak tables, hanging ferns, and teal-glazed plates coming out of a kitchen that never rushes you.

Locals come for the flat white before work. Everyone else stays for brunch and doesn't look at the clock again.
